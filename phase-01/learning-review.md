## Purpose

A charter agrees what problem the project investigates, what work belongs in scope and what evidence will count as completion. It prevents a proposed software solution from replacing a clear business need.

Your quality-audit experience is relevant: an error observation identifies a problem; a control addresses risk; evidence shows whether the control operated. A proposed cause still requires investigation.

## Worked example

NS-ORD-1001 requests 100 brackets. Only 60 are physically on hand, and their availability for this customer is unresolved. A possible error is promising all 100 for 9 October without checking commitments and restrictions. A proposed control is to validate available quantity and a defensible delivery date before confirming the promise. Evidence would include the availability information used, the recorded promise and who confirmed it. These are proposed controls and required evidence, not verified SAP functions or actual records.

An alternative is offering a partial delivery with customer agreement. That may meet an urgent customer need but add handling and transport effort. We have not decided whether it is the correct policy.

## Practical task

Write three sentences proposing a different possible error, a suitable control and evidence you would inspect. You may use missing customer information or a delayed billing handoff. Avoid confidential employer details.

## Three understanding-check questions

1. For NS-ORD-1001, why can we not promise 100 units merely because 60 units are physically in the warehouse? Name two facts you would check before confirming a delivery date.
2. Goods leave the warehouse on Monday, the goods-issue posting is recorded on Tuesday, and the customer receives them on Wednesday. Which event would you use to assess a promise of customer receipt, and why must the other dates remain separate?
3. Explain the difference between completing this portfolio and achieving a business improvement. Give one piece of evidence for each and identify which we currently have.

Review criteria: distinguish physical stock from available stock; distinguish dispatch, posting and receipt; distinguish portfolio artifacts from business outcomes. Learner responses and feedback remain pending. No pass or competence claim is recorded.
